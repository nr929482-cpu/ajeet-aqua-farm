About Ajeet Aqua Farm

Ajeet Aqua Farm is a fish farming business operated by Ajeet Kumar. We purchase small fish, grow them carefully in ponds, and supply grown fish to local traders and wholesale buyers.

Our Fish Farming Process

🐟 1. Small Fish

We purchase healthy small fish for pond farming.

🌊 2. Fish Farming

Fish are carefully grown and maintained in ponds.

📈 3. Growth

We take care of fish growth until they reach suitable size.

🚚 4. Wholesale Supply

Grown fish are supplied to local traders and wholesale buyers.
